Explanation of rowspan and colspan

  • rowspan="2" in Date: The same date applies to two examination rows, so one date cell is used for both rows instead of repeating it.
  • colspan="2" in Instructor Details: The heading covers two related columns: Instructor Name and Course Group.
  • colspan="6" in the footer: The reminder belongs to the complete table, so it stretches across all six columns.
